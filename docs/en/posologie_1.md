<span class="inline-block pb-2 mt-1 mb-2 border-b-4 border-yellow-300/50 font-medium">
  <span class="bg-green-200/50 px-1 py-1">PHASE 1 - PHYSICAL</span>: Water Fasting - 10 Days

- <span class="underline-text">Body preparation</span>: Induction of nutritional stress and purification (unassisted detoxification)

</span>

<div class="bg-green-200/50 px-1 py-1">OBJECTIVE:</div>

Predisposition to the natural balance of the organism through water detoxification - Fasting to induce <span id="autophagy-ref1">[Autophagy](/en/terminologie#autophagy){data-return="autophagy-ref1"}</span> (recycling of internal cellular components) and <span id="apoptosis-ref2">[Apoptosis](/en/terminologie#apoptosis){data-return="apoptosis-ref2"}</span> - Acid-base balance (weighted with disease); relative Homeostasis.

<div class="inline-block p-4 my-4 border-4 border-green-600/20 dark:border-green-200/50 rounded-lg italic text-center text-green-800 dark:text-green-200">
  <div class="prose dark:prose-invert">
<b>💡 Explanatory note!</b>: The induction of nutritional stress (through extreme fasting) modulates cellular behaviors, the body switches to a pragmatic survival mode. The scarcity of nutrients forces cells to share, to prioritize, they now have time to reorganize. Major recycling (phagocytosis), sacrifices (autophagy) take place as detoxification and survival. Biosynthesis regresses due to lack of energy input. Cell regeneration is no longer <span class="underline-text">quantitative</span> (decreased pace), and becomes <span class="underline-text">qualitative</span> (outputs are optimized based on availability and operational need), in short, we do less quickly, and much better... <b>All these new dispositions are devastating for the cancerous tumor, contributing unreservedly to <span class="underline-text">its progressive weakening</span> (through shortage) and to <span class="underline-text">the reduction of its volume</span> (through autophagy).</b>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="bg-green-200/50 px-1 py-1">ACTIONS:</div><br>

<span class="bg-blue-500 text-gray-50 px-1 py-1">Do a water fast</span> 🚿 More than 7 days ideally or minimum 3 days
- Drink only water. ⛔ <span class="text-red-300"><span id="imc-ref1">[BMI](/en/terminologie#bmi){data-return="imc-ref1"}</span> must be above 18.5</span>

<span class="bg-blue-500 text-gray-50 px-1 py-1">Or flexible water fasting</span>
- Take a broth / soup in the evening (300 to 1,100 kcal/day)

<div class="bg-green-200/50 px-1 py-1">CONTROL:</div>

- BMI and Subject fatigue state (add some protein if needed).
- Take pH (urinary) at beginning and end of phase.
- Take an image (markers) of tumor mass by ultrasound.

<div class="bg-green-200/50 px-1 py-1">DIET:</div>

Water fasting in <span id="regime-ref1">[Ketogenic Diet](/en/terminologie#regime){data-return="regime-ref1"}</span><br><br>

<b>💡 Explanatory note!</b>: The idea here is to take advantage of the vacant fasting time to focus the mind and make an introspective situation. After a while, when we have overcome the sensation of hunger, the body resigns itself to the new constraints. <span class="underline-text">Our mind</span> completely detaches from physical grip and subtracts from it — as for cells but on the psychic plane — it will now have time to regurgitate (missed acts, traumas, stress states, existential challenges and other toxicities), to isolate them, and to seek downstream (with a third person) to resolve the conflicts or discomforts that result... <b>In this upstream phase, it will be about highlighting all potential psychic conflict factors and identifying those responsible for organic conversion, that is, those psychosomatic, which will be used in developing the therapeutic plan.</b>
