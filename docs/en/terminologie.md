# Terminology

## Common Medical Terms {#medical-terms}

[Go to abbreviations](#abbreviations)

### Acid {#acid}
An environment is considered acidic when its pH is below 7. This acidity is due to a high concentration of hydronium ions (H₃O⁺) or protons (H⁺).
<ReturnLink />

### Angiogenesis {#angiogenesis}
The process by which new blood vessels form from pre-existing vessels.
<ReturnLink />

### Apoptosis {#apoptosis}
Programmed Cell Death.
<ReturnLink />

### Autophagy {#autophagy}
A physiological process by which a cell degrades and recycles its own internal components, such as misfolded proteins, damaged organelles, or protein aggregates.
<ReturnLink />

### Biosynthesis {#biosynthesis}
The process by which cells produce complex molecules essential for their structure and function from simple precursors. This mechanism is fundamental for the formation of organic compounds such as proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids.
<ReturnLink />

### VEGF {#vegf}
(Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor) is a glycoprotein essential in the process of angiogenesis.
<ReturnLink />

### Endosymbiosis {#endosymbiosis}
A form of symbiosis where an organism lives inside the cells or tissues of another organism.
<ReturnLink />

### Fermentation {#fermentation}
Metabolic process by which cells produce energy in the absence of oxygen (anaerobic), by breaking down organic molecules like glucose.
